Personal Experience in Grafton

John has lived in Grafton and works with buyers, sellers and investors throughout town and central Worcester County. Grafton's different sections—from North Grafton to Grafton Center and South Grafton—offer a mix of suburban neighborhoods, historic mill-village areas and more rural residential settings.

Transportation is also a major part of the town's appeal. Grafton has its own MBTA commuter rail station on the Worcester Line and convenient access to I-90, I-495, Route 20 and Routes 122 and 140.
