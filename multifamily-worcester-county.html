Worcester County Multifamily Real Estate

Hands-on representation for multifamily buyers, house hackers, investors and sellers throughout Worcester County.

2–4 Family

John analyzes 2–4 family properties based on the buyer's actual objective. An investor may focus on cash-on-cash return, cap rate, rents, expenses and upside, while a house hacker may care more about effective monthly housing cost, owner-occupied financing and how the property performs after moving out. The goal is to identify risk, upside and whether the investment is actually solid.

5+ Unit Multifamily

Larger multifamily requires a deeper look at the operating picture. John reviews income, expenses, rent roll, condition, financing and value-add opportunity and has transaction experience with larger multifamily, including a Park Street transaction.

Multifamily Sellers

For sellers, John coordinates with property management and tenants, organizes financial information, prepares P&L materials and packages the asset for investor buyers. Strategic pricing, comprehensive marketing and negotiation are all focused on presenting the property clearly and competing for top dollar.
