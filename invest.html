JOHN LESACK · REALTOR®

Multifamily & Investment Real Estate

Real estate investors need more than a property tour. John works with buyers and sellers across 2–4 family, 5+ unit multifamily, fix-and-flip and commercial opportunities.

2–4 Family

Every investment should be evaluated around the buyer's actual goal. A house hacker may care about effective monthly housing cost and future rental income, while an investor may focus more heavily on cash-on-cash return, cap rate, risk and upside. John helps analyze current and market rents, expenses, financing, condition and value-add potential so clients can understand how solid the investment really is.

5+ Unit Multifamily

For larger multifamily, John helps clients dig into rent roll, income, expenses, financing, condition and value-add potential. His transaction experience includes larger multifamily such as a Park Street sale.

Multifamily Sellers

For multifamily listings, John coordinates with property management and tenants, organizes income and expense information, prepares P&L materials and packages the property for investor buyers. The goal is to make the sale seamless for ownership while presenting the financial story clearly enough to compete for top dollar.

Fix & Flip

John helps buyers evaluate fixer-uppers through purchase price, rehab costs and after-repair value (ARV), and can connect clients with contractors and tradespeople. When the renovation is complete, JPL Real Estate can bring the finished property back to market with a seller strategy designed to maximize the resale.
